1. Functional Testing
Check that the pencil writes on standard paper.
Check how pencil writes, writes clear or not, consistency of the lines
Check eraser, how it is removing pencil marks on different surfaces, papers
Check the lead’s resistance to breakage under normal use.
Check if eraser can remove only pencil marks. (Mostly based on requirement)
2. Usability Testing
Check how comfortable the pencil is to hold.
Check weight during extended writing sessions.
Check the ease of switching between writing and erasing.
Check how comfortable for long use.
3. Performance Testing
Test the eraser's lifespan: how many erasures it can handle effectively.
Check durability of the pencil’s lead with repeated sharpening.
4. Load Testing
Check the pencil’s performance under heavy writing workloads (e.g., writing multiple pages continuously).
Check erasing large areas without tearing or damaging the paper.
5. Stress Testing
Test the lead’s resistance under heavy pressure.
Do drop tests to see if the pencil withstands impacts without breaking
6. Security Testing
Ensure that the materials used are non-toxic and safe for users.
Ensure that the pencil is safe for children (no sharp edges, small  parts).
7. Compatibility Testing
Test writing quality across different types of paper.
Test compatibility with various types of sharpeners.
Test eraser performance on different grades of pencil marks (HB, 2B, etc.).
